#2519. DFB Pokal Player Ratings | SG Sonnenhof Großaspach 2-4 Augsburg

Bundesliga DFB-Pokal participants Augsburg have survived a scare from 3. Liga opponents SG Sonnenhof Großaspach and advanced to the third round of the 2026/27 competition with a 2-4 away win at the WIRmachenDRUCK Arena. Großaspach put in a determined performance on their home turf, nearly knocking the Fuggerstädter out of the German cup in the second round for the second consecutive season. FCA head coach Manuel Baum nevertheless ensured that the favorites would prevail with a pair of astute substitutions.

The hosts made little attempt to press forward in the opening 10 minutes, sitting deep in a low block in the hopes of absorbing FCA pressure. Brave defending from the 3. Liga outfit kept the Bavarian Swabian guests without a shot on target for quite some time despite attempts to find a way through via virtually all angles. The “DorfKlub” were even able to send in an effort of their own via a Tim Rossmann set-piece in the 9th minute. Augsburg’s 65 percent possession certainly counted as one form of dominance, but hope remained alive.

The top-flight side eventually managed to secure the 1-0 in the 13th minute. Mert Kömür and Robin Fellhauer were involved in a move that saw striker Rodrigo Ribeiro eventually receive the ball from not far out centrally. SG94 keeper Maximilian Reule couldn’t get a good view through a maze of bodies in the box, enabling the Portuguese attacker to roll the ball in easily on the turn. To their immense credit, the underdog hosts quickly demonstrated that they had no intention of folding.

Pascal Reinhardt’s XI – unchanged since a heavy 1-7 defeat against SV Wehen Wiesbaden in their last 3. Liga match – began threatening along the right-hand side. In the 21st minute, SG94 midfielder Marius Kunde struck the post with a distance effort. The hosts truly appeared snake-bit when FCA defender Hennes Behrens spotted Ribeiro breaking upfield. Behrens connected with a beautiful ball. Ribeiro, just as casually as he did during his first goal, rolled the ball past helpless keeper Reule.

Once again threatened with demoralization, the proper Swabians refused to lie down. Less than a minute later, a quick throw-in from Niklas Pollex enabled left-back Luca Molinari to place the ball in the back of the Augsburg net. A capacity crowd of 10,000 at the WIRmachenDRUCK Arena waited with bated breath whilst a lengthy VAR review checked for a possible offside position. Match official Patrick Schwengers officially confirmed the 1-2 in the 24th minute, sending the locals and their ultra section into convulsions.

Sufficiently loosened up by the three goals, both teams showed little aversion to risk over the course of the rest of the opening half. The crowd found itself treated to some very entertaining football in which the ball swung from end to end. A clear-cut scoring opportunity may not have emerged for a while, but the optical quality of the football ensured that few spent much time sitting passively in their seats. Großaspach fullbacks Molinari and Rahn pressed exceedingly high, each generating half chances of their own.

Shortly before the end of the first 45, the hosts came close to equalizing twice. FCA keeper Finn Dahmen had to remain on his toes to parry a deflected effort in the 45th. One minute later, SG striker Pollex nearly bungled the ball over the line. Before it was time to head into the locker rooms, Augsburg’s Young-Woo Seol forced an excellent save out of keeper Reule at 45+2. The hosts were gradually working their way into the game. One had the sense that the second half had something special in store.

A corner two minutes after the restart enabled the underdogs to equalize. At first, nothing appeared to come of the 47th-minute dead ball. Lively SG94 fullback Luca Molinari was nevertheless able to get a second cross in. Großaspach skipper Volkan Celiktas rose highest to head the 2-2 home. The Bundesliga club, visibly rattled, struggled to mount a response. The hosts appeared content to allow the ball to circulate through the FCA ranks, but Baum’s charges couldn’t punch through with anything coherent.

The hour mark came and went with nothing to show for the Augsburg efforts beyond a couple of blocked shots. The Bundesliga trainer opted to freshen things up with the introduction of Arijon Ibrahimovic and Anton Kade in the 65th minute. Within ten minutes, both FCA subs got their names on the scoresheet. Juma Bah and Behrens were able to get a poorly fisted corner away to Ibrahimovic in the 70th. The Bayern Munich loanee finished from about 25 meters out with a curling effort into the right corner.

Four minutes after that, it was once again Behrens supplying the assist for Kade. The 74th-minute 4-2 came from close range after a short counter the leggy hosts simply weren’t able to cope with. From that point forward, it was the Bundesliga side that looked more likely to score. Keeper Reule denied Fellhauer a chance at the 5-2 in the 83rd. The hosts were able to put together a pair of decent attacking sorties before the full-time whistle blew, but the guests never truly relinquished control of the game.

#2518. "Lost" Barcelona star Lamine Yamal slammed by Spanish media after El Clasico defeat to Real Madrid

By Lewis Blain | 26 Oct 2026 07:01, Last updated: 26 Oct 2026 07:59

Barcelona suffered a frustrating 2-1 defeat to Real Madrid on Sunday night, as Kylian Mbappe scored twice to secure bragging rights for Jose Mourinho's side in El Clasico.

Raphinha briefly restored parity for Hansi Flick's men after Mbappe's opener, but the Frenchman struck again in the 87th minute to condemn Barcelona to their first defeat in LaLiga this season.

Lamine Yamal endured a difficult afternoon against Spain teammate Marc Cucurella, missing a golden opportunity before struggling to influence proceedings consistently.

The Spanish media were largely critical of the teenager's performance, though they acknowledged he remains one of Barcelona's most dangerous attacking players.

AS were particularly critical, publishing the headline "Lamine se bloqueo con Cucurella" - Lamine was shut down by Cucurella.

They felt that the Chelsea defender won their individual battle, leaving Yamal uncomfortable throughout the contest. The Barcelona winger continued to attempt to beat his opponent but lacked clarity in his decision-making, while also missing two excellent opportunities. AS concluded that an improved second-half showing was insufficient to earn a passing assessment.

La Vanguardia awarded Yamal 5/10, describing him as "Apagado", meaning subdued.

The report acknowledged that Yamal combined better with Xavi Espart than with Joao Cancelo, while recognising his attempts to fight back against the circumstances. However, the newspaper highlighted his glaring first-half miss, describing the opportunity as "infallible" - one that should have been scored.

El Nacional were more measured, giving the youngster 6/10 and labelling him "Frustrado", or frustrated.

The Catalan publication felt that Yamal produced a respectable performance without reaching his usual exceptional standards, explaining that he tried different ways to influence the game but was ultimately controlled too effectively by Madrid.

El Desmarque also awarded Yamal 6/10, publishing the headline "Un Lamine Yamal perdido" - a lost Lamine Yamal.

Their assessment acknowledged that he tried to create danger from the right, sought one-on-one situations and became increasingly involved as the match progressed. However, he lacked precision in decisive moments and could not turn his attacking efforts into a contribution that changed the outcome.

Mundo Deportivo were similarly unimpressed, describing the winger as "Disuelto", meaning subdued or fading out of the game. They acknowledged that he created dangerous moments and had a clear opportunity before half-time, but concluded that he failed to leave his mark on the contest.

Despite the criticism, Yamal still demonstrated glimpses of the quality during the 2-1 defeat that makes him such an important player for Barcelona.

Opta's post-match analysis recorded a team-high expected-goals (xG) figure of 0.51, although he failed to register a shot on target. His underlying threat suggests that his display was not entirely devoid of attacking promise.

Mourinho also praised the teenager after the match, describing him as "realmente bueno, muy bueno y muy habil" - really good, very good and very skilful. The Real Madrid boss acknowledged the challenge Yamal posed, while praising Cucurella for handling the winger so effectively.

There is little reason for Barcelona to panic over one difficult performance from a player who has repeatedly shown he can influence major matches. However, the 19-year-old star will need to rediscover his confidence and improve his decision-making in the final third, particularly when opponents deny him space to cut inside.

The defeat was a frustrating setback for Barcelona, but Yamal's best football is unlikely to be far away. With his individual quality still recognised even by Mourinho, the teenager has every opportunity to respond positively and remind supporters why he remains central to the club's ambitions this season.
